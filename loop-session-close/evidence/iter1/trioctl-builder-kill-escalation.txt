## Summary

Factored Claude's `_recorded_tmux_pid` / `_tmux_pane_pid` / `_kill_leftover_tmux_process` plus their two kill-verify timeout constants into a new focused `omnigent/native_tmux_kill.py` (147 lines, heavily commented). The new module exposes a combined `kill_session_with_escalation(bridge_dir, *, socket_path, tmux_target, kill_session_fn)` that resolves the pane pid (recorded in `tmux.json`, falling back to `tmux list-panes`), runs `kill-session` via the bridge's own `_run_tmux` callback (so each bridge's error messages stay authoritative), then SIGKILLs any leftover pane process group through `_proc._killpg` — the exact protocol Claude already used, no new kill protocol invented.

Claude's `kill_session` now calls the shared helper with behavior preserved (same call sequence: list-panes → kill-session → SIGKILL). Cursor and Codex `kill_session` previously did a plain `tmux kill-session`; they now follow Claude's pattern and escalate leftover pids too. No other bridges were churned.
